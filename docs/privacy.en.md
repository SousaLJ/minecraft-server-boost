# Privacy

The public repository does not need world data or credentials to diagnose most
errors.

## Local mod data

- `player_data.json` may contain UUIDs and names;
- `permissions.json` contains UUIDs, names, and administrative decisions;
- `skin_cache.json` contains names, UUIDs, and signed texture properties;
- `config.toml` may contain the MineSkin token;
- logs may contain IPs, names, local paths, and messages.

Create a copy, remove or replace these values, and only then attach the file.
Never sanitize the server's original file in place.

## Public retention

Content posted to Issues and Discussions may remain in history after editing.
If you expose a credential, revoke it immediately and notify the maintainer; do
not rely only on deleting the comment.
